Ableton Live 12 to 11 Beta

Check what's inside a Live set, and convert a Live 12 set so Live 11 can open it. Free, and your set stays on your computer.

Live 12 .als→Live 11
Choose set or drop an .als file here Your original set is not changed. Samples are not needed.

  • Files stay on your device
  • No sign-up
  • Free

Before you convert

  • This converter is in beta. Keep your original set and check the converted one in Live 11 before you rely on it.
  • Devices that only exist in Live 12 (for example Roar and Meld) are removed. Freeze and flatten those tracks in Live 12 first to keep their sound.
  • MIDI take lanes, tuning systems and some Live 12 view settings are dropped, because Live 11 has no place for them.
  • Copy your samples along with the set, or use Collect All and Save in Live 12 first.
