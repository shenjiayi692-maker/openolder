Why 2026 projects need more than a version change

The old trick was to unzip the .prproj, lower the version number and zip it again. Premiere Pro 2026 saves projects in a more compact form that leaves out fields older versions require, so that trick now gives a "project is damaged" error. This tool adds those fields back and then sets the version.
