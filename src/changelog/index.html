Changelog

OpenOlder runs in your browser, so every visitor always gets the latest version. Supported versions: projects from Premiere 27 and older can be converted to any older release back to CS4.

1.4 · 22 November 2026

  • Premiere 27 support: convert 27 projects and Productions to 2026 or older. Tested in real Premiere (27 to 2026).
  • Links to the open-source library behind the converter.

1.3 · 21 November 2026

  • Warning when a project comes from a Premiere release newer than the newest supported one (such as Premiere 27.0).
  • Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese versions of the main pages.
  • New help page for the "project could not be loaded, it may be damaged" error.
  • Anonymous usage counts (no files, file names or personal data; see About).

1.2 · 13 November 2026

  • About page, comparison of XML export with project conversion, and clearer page descriptions.

1.1 · 4 November 2026

  • Batch conversion of several projects at once, and whole Productions (folder or .zip).
  • Tested in real Premiere Pro: 2026 projects open in 2025 after conversion.

1.0 · 4 November 2026

  • First release: converts a Premiere Pro .prproj so an older version can open it, including 2026 projects (restores the fields 2026 leaves out). Browser port of the open-source prem-down.
  • Ableton Live 12 to 11 converter (beta, non-commercial).
